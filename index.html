02 / Deep Dives
Case 01 · Analytics · Retention

Finding the real leak before buying more traffic.

Revenue had stopped growing and the obvious answer was "get more signups". Before spending on that, I joined billing, product analytics and search data to see where customers were actually lost.

What I did

  • Built monthly cohorts from billing data and followed every one through its first renewals.
  • Compared users who got a free trial with users who didn't, and checked how many ever reached one.
  • Caught my own wrong finding: a "bad February cohort" was just February having 28 days.

What it changed

  • The leak was at first renewal, not at the top of the funnel. Every cohort lost a large share there.
  • Trial users converted several times better, but only a small share of signups ever found the trial.
  • That became the plan: activation emails, a clearer trial path and new pricing, before any ad spend.

Case 03 · SEO · Multi-agent pipeline

A content team made of agents, with a human at the gate.

After writing most of the site's guides by hand, I turned the process into a pipeline: four specialized agents that research, brief, outline and draft, each checked by an independent verifier.

How it works

  • Planner pulls search results and Google's AI Overview, then writes the brief.
  • A reader-goal registry checks the new article doesn't compete with an old one.
  • Outline and draft agents follow written rules; a verifier patches what they miss.

What it gave us

  • Idea to full draft in about 20 minutes, with keyword, linking and verification reports.
  • Merging two competing articles into one pillar more than doubled that page's clicks.
  • 87 reports logged since launch, so every decision on a page can be traced back.

Operating Principles

05 / Approach
i.

Find the leak first

More traffic into a leaking funnel is wasted money. I look at where customers drop before I spend on getting more.

ii.

Ship it myself

If an idea needs a page, a tool or an email, I build it the same week and test it, instead of writing a spec and waiting.

iii.

Agents do the volume

AI does the repetitive work, and I make the calls. The skill is deciding where that line sits.

iv.

Check my own numbers

I try to break my findings before I present them. My best catch so far: a "bad cohort" that was only a short month.
