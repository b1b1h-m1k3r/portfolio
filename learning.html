How I got here

From zero to systems,
and how you could too.

Four years ago I had never touched a computer. I ran a small local business on one-to-one, face-to-face sales. Then I heard about AI, and something clicked. What follows is the honest map of how I taught myself: the order it actually happened in, and the exact sources I leaned on along the way. They are all free and public. If you are starting where I started, the same doors are open to you.

The path

01 / Where I started
2022

The starting point

A local business run entirely on one-to-one, in-person sales. No computer, no code, no online presence. Everything was conversation and handshake.

2023

The spark

I heard about AI for the first time and was immediately hooked. I knew I had to start. The same year I launched an HR-marketing agency, my first real reason to get on a computer every day.

2024

First real tools

I started working in Notion and Make.com and using GPT day to day, running the agency on them. This is where automation and AI stopped being ideas and became how I actually worked.

2025

All in on systems

I left the agency behind and specialized in marketing systems. This is where I started taking agent architectures seriously and went deep, designing the multi-stage, verified pipelines I build today.

2026 · today

Designing and shipping systems

Multi-agent AI pipelines with verification gates, no-code automations, bespoke AI skills, and the design-ops work documented in the portfolio, built to run with minimal hand-holding.
